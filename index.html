I owe you an apology, Simran.

I'm not going to make excuses. My anger is my problem to fix, not yours to deal with. I'm sorry. Truly.

You don't have to reply, and you don't have to say it's okay. Take all the time you need. I just wanted you to know that I see it, and I'm working on it.
