The ride I'll never forget

Yesterday I dropped you home. Normal day, normal road. Or so I thought.

Then you hugged me from behind. And something strange happened.

Everything I'd been carrying went quiet. The stress, the overthinking, the past, whatever the future is planning. All of it, just… gone.

No yesterday. No tomorrow. Just the road, the wind, and your arms around me.

For those few minutes the whole world was two people on one bike. Just you and me. Nothing else existed.

I've ridden that road so many times. Yesterday was the first time I didn't want it to end.

So if you ever wonder what you mean to me, it's that. You make everything else disappear. 💗

— Dhruv
